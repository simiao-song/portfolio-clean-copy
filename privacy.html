Privacy & visit statistics.

Last updated November 16, 2026.

This portfolio uses optional Google Analytics to help me understand how people find the site and which projects they explore. Analytics loads only after you choose “Allow.” Choosing “No thanks” lets you use the entire website without loading Analytics.

What the reports show

Visit dates, page views, project openings, document downloads, traffic sources, browser and device types, and approximate geographic locations. These are website activity reports; I do not use them to identify visitors by name, employer, or professional background.

Google processes the connection information needed to deliver Analytics, including an IP address to derive approximate location. Google Analytics does not give me a list of visitors’ raw IP addresses. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled on this site.

Your choice

Allowing Analytics permits its first-party measurement cookies. Your preference is remembered in this browser for up to six months. You can reopen “Privacy settings” in the website footer at any time and choose “No thanks” to stop further measurement and remove Analytics cookies for this site. Browser settings and extensions may also prevent measurement.

The site does not send email form entries or other submitted personal details to Analytics. Email links open your own email application. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure may process connection data independently of this optional Analytics choice.

For details about Google’s processing, see Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Analytics data safeguards.
